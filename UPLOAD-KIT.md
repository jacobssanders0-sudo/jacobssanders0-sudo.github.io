## Book 1 — Pip the Little Firefly (English edition, paperback)

1. Go to **https://kdp.amazon.com/bookshelf** and sign in.
2. Click **+ Create** (top right), then choose **Paperback** → **Create paperback**.

### Page 1 of the form — "Paperback Details"

3. **Language:** English
4. **Book title** — paste exactly:
   ```
   Pip the Little Firefly
   ```
5. **Subtitle** — paste exactly:
   ```
   A Gentle Story About Being Brave and Kind
   ```
6. **Series / Edition number:** leave both blank.
7. **Author** — First name `Jacob`, Middle name `Stetson`, Last name `Sanders`.
   (This prints as "Jacob Stetson Sanders," matching the cover.)
8. **Contributors:** skip.
9. **Description** — paste exactly:
   ```
   Pip is a little firefly who is afraid of the dark — and cannot glow. When his friend Luna the moth is lost one night, Pip takes a deep breath, remembers to be brave and be kind, and lights her way home. A gentle read-aloud story for ages 3-6 about courage and helping a friend, with bright illustrations on every page and simple words perfect for early readers.
   ```
10. **Publishing rights:** choose **"I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights."**
11. **Keywords** — one per box, paste each exactly:
    ```
    firefly bedtime story for kids ages 3-6
    afraid of the dark children's book
    kindness and courage picture book
    read aloud story for preschoolers
    early reader simple words picture book
    tennessee nature bedtime story for kids
    feelings and friendship picture book
    ```
12. **Categories** — click "Choose categories" and pick these two:
    - Juvenile Fiction → Animals
    - Juvenile Fiction → Social Themes → Emotions & Feelings
13. **Age and grade range:** set **Minimum age 3**, **Maximum age 6** (years). Leave grade range blank if it asks.
14. **Pre-order:** choose **"I am ready to release my book."**
15. Click **Save and Continue**.

### Page 2 of the form — "Paperback Content"

16. **ISBN:** choose **"Get a free KDP ISBN"** and accept the one it assigns. (The imprint/publisher field can stay blank.)
17. **Publication date:** leave blank (it fills in at release).
18. **Print options:**
    - Ink and paper type: **Premium color interior with white paper**
    - Trim size: **8.5 × 8.5 in** (if it isn't listed, click "Select a different size" and enter 8.5 × 8.5)
    - Bleed settings: **No Bleed**
    - Paperback cover finish: **Glossy**
19. **Manuscript (interior file):** click **Upload paperback manuscript** and choose **book.pdf** (the English interior from Step 0). Wait for the green "processed" checkmark.
20. **Book cover:** choose **"Use Cover Creator"** (do NOT choose "Upload a cover you already have" — that option needs a full wrap-around PDF we didn't make).
    - In Cover Creator, pick any simple template, then click the front-cover image and choose **upload / replace image** → select **cover.jpg** from Step 0. The artwork already contains the title and author name, so if the template adds its own title text on top, delete the template's text boxes so nothing is doubled.
    - For the **back cover**, paste this text into the back-cover text box:
      ```
      Pip is afraid of the dark — and he cannot glow. But when his friend Luna is lost one night, Pip takes a deep breath… and tries. A gentle read-aloud story about courage and helping a friend, for ages 3–6.
      ```
    - Click **Preview** in Cover Creator, make sure the front looks clean, then **Save / approve** to exit Cover Creator.
21. **AI-generated content disclosure** — KDP asks whether the book contains AI-generated content. Answer **Yes**, and check:
    - **Text: AI-generated** ✔
    - **Images: AI-generated** ✔
    (This declaration goes to Amazon only; nothing is printed in the book.)
22. Click **Launch Previewer** and flip through the pages. Everything should look like the PDF you downloaded.
    - **Page count: confirmed 26 pages** — the interior now includes a title page, a dedication page, a "Words to Know" glossary, counting and maze activity pages, two coloring pages, and a draw-your-own page around the original story, so it clears KDP's 24-page paperback minimum with room to spare.
23. Click **Save and Continue**.

### Page 3 of the form — "Paperback Rights & Pricing"

24. **Territories:** choose **All territories (worldwide rights)**.
25. **Primary marketplace:** Amazon.com.
26. **List price:** enter **$9.99**. (The royalty line should show 60% minus printing cost — a few dollars per copy. That's expected for full color.)
27. Leave **expanded distribution** checked if offered.
28. Click **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

Amazon reviews new books before they go live — usually 24–72 hours. You'll get an email when it's live, and the Bookshelf status will change to **LIVE**.

## Book 2 — Pip the Little Firefly / Pipo la luciérnaga (Bilingual edition, paperback)

Same clicks as Book 1. Go back to the **Bookshelf → + Create → Paperback**, and use these values where they differ:

**Details page**
- **Language:** English (the book is bilingual; English is the primary listing language)
- **Title** — paste exactly:
  ```
  Pip the Little Firefly
  ```
- **Subtitle** — paste exactly:
  ```
  A Bilingual English-Spanish Story / Un cuento bilingüe inglés-español
  ```
- **Author:** Jacob Stetson Sanders (same as Book 1)
- **Description** — paste exactly:
  ```
  Pip is a little firefly who is afraid of the dark — and Pipo es una pequeña luciérnaga que le teme a la oscuridad. When his friend Luna the moth is lost one night, Pip takes a deep breath, remembers to be brave and be kind — sé valiente, sé amable — and lights her way home. Every page pairs the English and Spanish text side by side, with bright illustrations and simple words perfect for young bilingual readers ages 3-6.
  ```
- **Publishing rights:** "I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights."
- **Keywords** — one per box:
  ```
  bilingual children's book english spanish
  libro bilingüe para niños inglés español
  firefly bedtime story luciérnaga cuento
  spanish english picture book ages 3-6
  learn spanish story for kids
  cuento para dormir niños bilingüe
  kindness courage libro infantil
  ```
- **Categories** — pick these three:
  - Juvenile Fiction → Animals
  - Juvenile Fiction → Social Themes → Emotions & Feelings
  - Foreign Language Study → Spanish
- **Age range:** Minimum 3, Maximum 6. **Pre-order:** "I am ready to release my book." → **Save and Continue**.

**Content page**
- **ISBN:** Get a free KDP ISBN (it will be different from Book 1's — that's correct).
- **Print options:** Premium color interior with white paper · Trim **8.5 × 8.5 in** · **No Bleed** · **Glossy** finish.
- **Manuscript:** upload **book-bilingual.pdf** (from Step 0).
- **Cover:** Cover Creator again, same **cover.jpg** artwork. Back-cover text — paste exactly:
  ```
  Pip is afraid of the dark — and he cannot glow. Pipo le teme a la oscuridad — y no puede brillar. But when a friend is lost one night, Pip takes a deep breath… and tries. A gentle bilingual read-aloud about courage and helping a friend, for ages 3–6. / Un cuento bilingüe y tierno sobre la valentía y la amistad, para niños de 3 a 6 años.
  ```
- **AI-generated content disclosure:** answer **Yes** and check **Text**, **Images**, and **Translation** (the Spanish text was AI-translated — KDP counts that too).
- **Previewer:** flip through; this interior is also a **confirmed 26 pages**, above the 24-page minimum. → **Save and Continue**.

**Rights & Pricing page**
- All territories · Amazon.com · **$9.99** → **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

## Optional, later — the Kindle edition

Skip this on upload day unless you feel like it; it can be added any time from the Bookshelf ("Create Kindle edition" next to each paperback). The Kindle edition has **no page-count minimum** (the paperbacks are 26 pages now, so the minimum no longer binds either format). If you create it: same titles, descriptions, keywords, categories, and AI disclosure as above, and price it **$2.99–$4.99** (inside the 70% royalty band — $3.99 is the sweet spot).
