FAQ

What formats can I send for transcription?

Any common audio or video format (MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, MOV), or a link to the file. If I cannot open it, I will tell you before the order starts.

What is the difference between clean and full verbatim?

Clean verbatim removes fillers ("um", "uh"), stutters, and false starts for a readable transcript. Full verbatim keeps everything, exactly as spoken.

Can you label speakers by name?

Yes — list the names in your inquiry and I will use them; otherwise speakers are numbered.

What formats do you accept for data cleaning?

Excel (XLSX/XLS), CSV, TSV, Google Sheets links, and PDF for extraction packages.

Will my data be changed in ways I did not ask for?

No. Only the fixes in your package are applied, untouched columns are checksum-verified, and the report lists every change class.

Is my data confidential?

It is used only for your job and never shared, sold, or reused. The same applies to transcription files and manuscripts.

Which trim sizes can you format for?

Any standard KDP/IngramSpark size; 6x9 is the most common. Tell me yours in the inquiry.

What if KDP rejects my file?

Platform-checker fixes are free within your revision rounds.

What data will you collect from the web?

Only data that any visitor can see without an account — prices, product listings, and public business information. I check each site's terms before I start; if a site does not allow automated collection, I will tell you up front and decline that part of the job. I do not collect personal contact details for private individuals, and nothing I deliver comes from behind a login or a paywall.
